Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2025

We keep things simple at B-PlanDIY. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we keep it safe. We comply with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

1. Who we are

B-PlanDIY is a New Zealand-based online tool that helps small business owners and startups create a business plan and cashflow forecast. You can contact us at support@b-plandiy.com.

2. What information we collect

We collect only what we need to provide our service:

  • Business plan information — everything you enter into the app (business details, financials, goals etc) is saved locally on your own device using your browser's local storage. This information is never sent to or stored on our servers.
  • Feedback — if you choose to send us feedback through the app, we collect your star rating and any comments you provide. This is sent to us via email.
  • Payment information — when you purchase access, your payment is processed by our payment provider. We do not store your credit card or payment details.
  • WhatsApp group — if you choose to join our private WhatsApp community, you share your name and phone number voluntarily with the group.
  • Email address — if you contact us directly or sign up for seminars, we may collect your email address to respond to you.

3. How we use your information

  • To provide access to the B-PlanDIY tool
  • To respond to your feedback or support requests
  • To invite you to our WhatsApp community and business planning seminars
  • To process your payment

We do not sell your information to anyone. We do not use it for advertising.

4. Your business plan data

The information you enter into the B-PlanDIY app — your business details, financials, goals and strategy — is stored entirely on your own device. It never leaves your browser. We cannot access it, and it is not stored on any of our servers. This means there are no privacy or security risks associated with the information you enter into the app.

5. Cookies

Our website may use basic cookies to help the site function. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

6. Third parties

We use a small number of trusted third-party services:

  • Payment provider — to process your one-time payment securely
  • Formspree — to receive feedback submitted through the app
  • WhatsApp — for our community group (subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy)

7. Keeping your information safe

We take reasonable steps to protect any personal information we hold. As most of your data stays on your own device, the main risk is with you — we recommend keeping your device secure.

8. Your rights

Under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020, you have the right to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you
  • Request corrections to any inaccurate information
  • Ask us to delete your information

To exercise any of these rights, email us at support@b-plandiy.com.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please get in touch:

Flowly Ltd
Business Plan and Cashflow Forecast
18 July 2025

1. Business Details

Flowly Ltd is a New Zealand-registered SaaS company at the early customer stage, developing a simple invoicing and cashflow management tool designed exclusively for sole traders and freelancers. Founded by a team with deep experience in both software development and small business operations, Flowly addresses a clear gap in the market: the absence of an affordable, genuinely simple invoicing tool built for the NZ context.

2. Market and Customers

The target market is New Zealand sole traders aged 25–50, a segment of over 350,000 businesses currently underserved by enterprise accounting platforms. The NZ SME accounting software market is estimated at approximately $120M annually.

Competitive advantage: Unlike Xero or MYOB — which are built for accountants — Flowly is designed to be used by the business owner directly, with no accounting knowledge required.

3. Vision and Goals

Vision: By 2027, to be the go-to invoicing tool for 10,000 NZ sole traders.

  • Reach 500 paying customers within 12 months
  • Achieve $10,000 MRR by end of Year 1
  • Launch mobile app by Q3 2025

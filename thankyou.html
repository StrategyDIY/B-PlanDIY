Important — one quick setup step

Your business plan is saved in this browser on this device rather than on our servers — but that also means your work will be permanently lost if your browser is set to clear site data when you close it.

Please check this setting before you start, and add b-plandiy.com as an exception.

Microsoft Edge — how to check
  1. Click the ⋯ menu (top right) → Settings
  2. Choose Privacy, search, and services
  3. Scroll to Clear browsing data
  4. Click Choose what to clear every time you close the browser
  5. If Cookies and other site data is turned on, either switch it off, or click Add next to "Do not clear" and enter b-plandiy.com
Google Chrome — how to check
  1. Click the ⋮ menu (top right) → Settings
  2. Choose Privacy and security → Third-party cookies
  3. Under Sites allowed to use cookies, click Add and enter b-plandiy.com
  4. Also check Privacy and security → Delete browsing data → On exit settings if you have them enabled
Safari — how to check
  1. Safari clears data from sites you haven't visited in 7 days when Prevent cross-site tracking is on
  2. Open the app at least once a week, or use Export data in the app to keep a backup file
  3. Avoid Private Browsing windows — nothing is saved in those

Whichever browser you use, please also avoid private/incognito windows, and use the Export data button in the app to save a backup file once you've entered your information.
